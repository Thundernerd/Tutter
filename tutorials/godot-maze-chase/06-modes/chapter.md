---
title: Modes
summary: Scatter and chase, the ghost house, frightened ghosts and eyes going home.
---

A ghost is always in one of a few situations, and each one changes how it moves and what it aims for:

| Situation | Moves | Aims for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| In the house | bobs up and down | nothing yet |
| Leaving | walks out through the door | the spot above the door |
| Scatter | the usual rule | its own corner |
| Chase | the usual rule | its personal chase target |
| Frightened | random turns, slower | nothing |
| Eaten | the usual rule, very fast | the ghost house |
