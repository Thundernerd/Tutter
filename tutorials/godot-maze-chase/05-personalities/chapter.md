---
title: Ghost personalities
summary: Four targeting strategies, and why simple rules beat perfect pathfinding.
---

All four ghosts use the movement rule from the previous chapter. The only difference between them is **which tile they aim for** in chase mode:

| Ghost | Colour | Nickname | Chase target |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Ember | red | the chaser | the player's tile |
| Rosie | pink | the ambusher | four tiles in front of the player |
| Frost | cyan | the flanker | the player's tile, mirrored around Ember |
| Pumpkin | orange | the shy one | the player when far away, its corner when close |
