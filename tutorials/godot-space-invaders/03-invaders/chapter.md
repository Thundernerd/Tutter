---
title: The invaders
summary: One invader scene, 55 instances built from code, and the march that speeds up.
---

The formation is the star of the game. Its rules are simple, and every one of them matters:

1. The whole grid moves sideways in small **steps**, all invaders at once.
2. When the next step would cross the screen edge, the grid **drops** down a row instead and **reverses**.
3. The fewer invaders remain, the **faster** it steps.
