---
title: Camera and head-to-head
summary: A shared camera that follows the leader and zooms to fit, and the head-to-head mode where falling off the screen costs you a point.
---

Micro Machines famously doesn't split the screen. Everybody shares one view, and in multiplayer that becomes the game: the camera follows whoever's in front, and if you drop far enough behind to fall off the edge, you lose the round. This chapter builds that camera and that mode.
