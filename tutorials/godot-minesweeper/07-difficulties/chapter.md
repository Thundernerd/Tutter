---
title: Difficulties
summary: Beginner, intermediate, expert and custom boards, scaled to fit the screen, picked from a menu.
---

Minesweeper has had the same three difficulties since the early 1990s:

| Difficulty | Size | Mines | Mine density |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Beginner | 9 × 9 | 10 | 12% |
| Intermediate | 16 × 16 | 40 | 16% |
| Expert | 30 × 16 | 99 | 21% |

Density, not size, is what makes a board hard: at 21%, expert boards force far more guesses than beginner ones. Custom boards let players pick any size and count they like, within limits.

Three problems to solve: the chosen difficulty has to survive the switch from the menu scene to the game scene, boards from 5×5 up to 40×20 all have to fit on a 640×360 screen, and players need a screen to choose from.
