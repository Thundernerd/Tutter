---
title: The HUD
summary: A mine counter, a timer, and a restart button with a face that reacts to the game.
---

The classic Minesweeper window has three things above the board: mines left on the left, seconds elapsed on the right, and a smiley in the middle that doubles as the restart button. We'll build all three, styled like digital displays with a **theme type variation**, and with a smiley drawn entirely in code.
